### Flexible cover to protect your car

Getting car insurance means you can get the right cover to suit you and your car. If something accidental should happen to your car and it’s covered by your policy, you can trust us to help you get your car back on the road as soon as possible.

We have a choice of covers ranging from full comprehensive to basic third party insurance.

### This might suit you if…

- you’re looking for a trusted insurer
- you want guaranteed quality repairs should something happen to your car
- you want a reliable claims experience.

### Here’s what you get:

- Cover to repair or replace your car if damaged or written off in an accident.
- Cover for windscreen breakage with no excess and without loss of your no claims discount (where this is the only damage).
- Emergency costs following an accident, to a maximum of $500.
