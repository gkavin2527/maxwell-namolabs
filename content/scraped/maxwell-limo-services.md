**Wine tasting tour / Tour cost NZ $230.00 (4 persons max)** -For the people who love wine can have taste of fine New Zealand wines produced in famous Kumeu region about 40 km out of Auckland. We collect you from your hotel at 10.00 am take you to four famous wineries for wine tasting. Then we take you to Muriwai beach known for black sand and return you back to your hotel at approx 2.00 pm.

**Tour of Auckland / Tour cost NZ $160.00 (4 persons max) –** We do Auckland sightseeing tour covering main attractions Within Auckland. We pick you up from your hotel and drop you back. This tour takes about 5 hours and covers Harbour Bridge. Mount Eden, Cornwall park, Parnell Village, Sky Tower, Queen street, Auckland Museum. Very Economical for 3-4 people.?First half day tour starts 8.30 am and ends at 1.30 pm , second half day tour from 2.00pm to 7.00pm.

**Waitomo Glow worm Caves Half day tour /Tour cost NZ $450.00 (4 persons max**) -Visit the world famous glow worm caves. Pick up from your hotel at 6.00am driving through the beautiful Waikato region reach Waitomo around 8.30 am. Leave Waitomo after seeing the caves to return to Auckland around 2.00pm.

**Rotorua day tour /Tour cost NZ $550.00 (4 persons max) -Rotorua** is built geo-thermal field and is worth exploring the bubbling mud pools, Maori village and various lakes in the area. Customize your tour according to what you would want to see and the time you would like to spend on each place of interest. Pick up from your hotel 6.00am and return to Auckland at 7.00pm.

**Bay of Islands(Pahia) day tour /Tour cost NZ $550.00 (4 persons max) -Bay of Islands** is known as the birthplace of the nation as the ‘Treaty of Waitangi’ was signed in this very region. There are many historic sites and old buildings in this region. Visit Hole in the rock, see the Dolphins or swim with them, Visit Waitangi ground. This is a tour worth doing when visiting New Zealand.
